**TalkHelper** (助听字幕) turns an Android tablet or phone into a caption screen. It listens to nearby Mandarin speech and shows it in very large, high-contrast Chinese text, so an older person with hearing loss can follow a conversation without a hearing aid.

[中文介绍 →](/talkhelper)

## Features

- Large white-on-black captions with only the last few lines on screen
- Offline Mandarin recognition on the device; no account, no network permission
- Bracketed cues for sounds such as laughter, doorbells, phone rings and barking dogs
- Review earlier lines while recognition keeps running
- Two models: a more accurate one and a faster one for old devices
- Dedicated-device mode: an old tablet boots straight into captions
- Android 8.0 and later, including 32-bit tablets

## Privacy

Audio never leaves the device and is not stored by default. See the [privacy policy](/talkhelper/privacy).
