Plain truth: the subscription didn't sell. Developer tools don't need upgrades urgently, and in the AI era you fix small bugs yourself. But rebuilding a framework as useful as My AI Team from scratch doesn't pay off, in time alone, never mind the tokens. So no more subscription: buy it once, with confidence and no strings attached.
