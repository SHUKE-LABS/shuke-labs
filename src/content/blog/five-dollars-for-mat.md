Five dollars can't buy you a loss. Five dollars can't buy you a con. But five dollars buys you `mat`, upgraded free for as long as it ships.

## What changed

My AI Team (`mat`) no longer rents by the year. It sells a Personal Copy: pay once, keep it.

- **$5 today, $1 more each month.** The price started at $5 on 12 November 2026 and rises $1 on the 12th of every month (UTC): $6 from 12 December. Whoever comes first pays least.
- **Upgrades for life.** `mat upgrade` pulls every release we ship. No renewal, no expiry.
- **One copy, one machine.** Moving machines? Run `mat license deactivate` on the old one and `mat activate` on the new.

Buy it at [mat.shukelabs.com/buy](https://mat.shukelabs.com/buy). Checkout runs on LemonSqueezy, our merchant of record.

## Why not a subscription

A developer tool is hard to rent. The copy you have keeps working whether you upgrade or not, and anyone with a coding agent can patch a small bug on their own. So we stopped renting it and started selling it, and let the price reward the people who show up early.

## What five dollars buys

`mat` runs structured AI-agent sessions against your GitHub repo from tmux. Agents claim ready tickets, get a plan approved by an independent reviewer, implement on a branch, open a PR, pass review by an agent that did not write the code, and merge only on green tests. You talk to them over Telegram instead of watching panes; they come to you for real decisions, not routine ones.

shuke's own pitch: you focus on requirements and acceptance; the AI team focuses on delivery and quality.
