# Refund policy — My AI Team

We want to make the world a better place, and that starts with how we treat
the people who buy from us. You should never feel stuck with a purchase that
isn't right for you.

## The 14-day promise

Every My AI Team purchase comes with a **14-day money-back guarantee**. If My
AI Team isn't right for you, request a refund within 14 days of purchase, for
any reason, and we will refund it in full. No questions, no hoops.

We'd rather refund a sale than keep someone's money for a product they don't
find useful.

## How refunds work

Payments are processed by **LemonSqueezy**, which is the merchant and seller of
record for every purchase. Refunds are handled through LemonSqueezy's refund
flow: use the receipt emailed to you at purchase, or email us and we'll help
you resolve it.

## After a refund

When a refund is issued, your licence to use the software ends. That's the
fair trade: you get your money back, we get the access back.

## Cancelling a subscription

As a subscription, your plan can be cancelled at any time to stop future
renewals. Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not by itself refund the
current period.

## If you're not sure

Questions? Email us — we're happy to talk before you buy.
